The story inside the news.

iSun1 turns sourced events into stories with a pulse. AI helps research and write; every published claim needs a source. Dates are event dates, not manufactured freshness.

This first edition runs two headline framings per story. Editorial scores are hypotheses. The Pulse shows observed reader signals, including when there is too little evidence to learn anything.

With your permission, a random first-party cookie remembers your experiment assignment for 30 days. We store anonymous story interactions, language and referral category. We do not store your raw IP address, name, email or browsing history elsewhere. Short-lived, hashed network buckets limit automated abuse. Sharing controls measure intent and handoff, not confirmed posts.

“Signals off” deletes this browser’s stored interactions and cookie. You can keep reading either way. The service host may retain its standard operational logs.
