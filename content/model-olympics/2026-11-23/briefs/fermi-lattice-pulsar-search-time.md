# A hidden rhythm in fifteen years of data

A study published November19,2026 reports the discovery of isolated gamma-ray pulsar PSR J1622−7204, spinning about261times per second, in fifteen years of older Fermi telescope data. A lattice-based method used about ten CPU hours; a previous semicoherent search failed after roughly600 CPU years. CPU time measures aggregate processor work, not elapsed historical years.

The method searches selected subsets of eighty weighted photons, rejects unphysical possibilities and checks candidate periodicity with larger datasets. The correct solution is not simply the shortest geometric vector. The full parameter estimation used15,905 weighted photon records, including background. The demonstrated improvement is case-specific; new binary-system discoveries remain a goal with additional computational demands.
