# Two different ledgers at COP31’s close

In his official November22,2026 closing speech in Antalya, UN Climate Change Executive Secretary Simon Stiell reported that issues related to both presidency-led decisions had remained without agreement. He also reported other negotiating decisions, including on a just transition, and progress through the voluntary Action Agenda. These statements establish his assessment, rather than every delegation’s position or a complete legal record.

The November19 Electrification Delivery Package announcement combines earlier reported deployment and future plans. UNEZA members reported more than21,000km of grid built in2025, separately from annual investment plans of at leastUS$156billion across grids, renewables and storage. These quantities differ in period, unit and completion status. Participating cities and businesses do not constitute a new binding global treaty; financial announcements should not be summed into one new fund.
