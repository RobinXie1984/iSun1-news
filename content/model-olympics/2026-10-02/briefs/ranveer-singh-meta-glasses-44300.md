# Ranveer Singh fronts Meta's India glasses launch; the agent comes later
Announcement date: October 1, 2026. Verified October 2, 2026 Hong Kong time.

[c1 / s1] Meta announced Ranveer Singh as the first India brand ambassador for Ray-Ban and Ray-Ban Meta, with its Frame Your True Self campaign. The release announces Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 availability in India starting at INR 44,300. No endorsement fee or sales result is disclosed.
[c2 / s1] Meta lists 27 color/lens combinations across Aviator, Zena and Wayfarer, a customizable action button and up to nine hours of battery life for Gen 3. Its performance statements are manufacturer claims, not independent tests.
[c3 / s1] Gen 3 is described as having a 12-megapixel camera, 3K video and an LED intended to alert people nearby when photos or video are taken. Meta says its six-microphone array reduces more than 90% of background noise. The LED description is not proof of universal bystander consent or privacy protection; the noise figure is not a promise to silence the wearer's surroundings.
[c4 / s1] A separate Ray-Ban Meta Audio product is described as coming soon: 43 grams, claimed up to 12 hours on a charge plus up to 48 additional hours from its case. Do not transfer those figures to Gen 3 or claim Audio is already available in India.
[c5 / s1] Meta says its personal agent Muse will come to its AI glasses in the coming months, with voice activation and actions related to what the wearer sees. This is future availability and claimed capability, not a verified feature on today's buyer's glasses.

Boundary: keep Gen 3, Audio and future Muse distinct. Do not claim phone replacement, all-day performance for every use, an independent benchmark, covert filming, proven sales driven by Singh, an endorsement price, or current Muse access. Starting price is Indian rupees, not U.S. dollars; no currency conversion is supplied.
