# Taylor Swift takes three of Britain’s top five singles as Showgirl returns
Event: UK Official Charts week October 2–8, 2026; verified October 3, 2026 Hong Kong time. The chart compiler is the primary source for rankings. These are UK charts, not global or U.S. Billboard rankings.

- c1 — Official Charts lists Patient Zero debuting at No.1, Cleveland at No.3 and Babylon at No.4: Taylor Swift has three of this week’s top five singles. That is three chart positions, not 60% of all listening or sales. [S1,S2]
- c2 — The compiler says Patient Zero is her eighth UK No.1 single and third of 2026, following Opalite and I Knew It, I Knew You. It says she is the only artist with three UK No.1 singles in 2026 so far. These three annual No.1 songs are different from the three current top-five songs. [S1]
- c3 — Following its expanded The Encore edition, The Life Of A Showgirl returns to the UK albums No.1 for its fifth non-consecutive week there, paired with Patient Zero atop singles. The original album was released October 3, 2025; this week's return is around its first anniversary, not a claim that the new chart began October3. [S1]
- c4 — Official Charts says Swift has achieved the album-and-single No.1 double in each calendar year from 2022 through 2026: five distinct pairings. That does not mean five uninterrupted years at No.1 or five new albums this week. [S1]
- c5 — The Official Singles Chart incorporates audio/video streams, downloads, CDs and vinyl. Chart positions are not raw sales counts, unique listeners, profit, personal income or proof of musical quality. [S2]

Boundaries: No current-week unit total, song revenue, endorsement income, fan motive, private relationship detail, lyrics or quotation is supplied. Do not infer everyone likes the music, critical reception, manipulation, mass repeat-buying or a causal marketing mechanism from rankings. The album’s fifth week at No.1 is non-consecutive. Commercial interpretation must be marked as analysis. Do not confuse UK download-only or Irish charts with the main UK singles chart.
