# Elon Musk’s Tesla: 486,532 deliveries, with profit still to come
Event: October 2, 2026 company release covering Q3 2026. Verified October 3, 2026 Hong Kong time. Tesla identifies Elon Musk as its CEO in its 2026 corporate filing; this story concerns company-reported operating figures, not a new personal statement by Musk.

- c1 — Tesla reported 464,391 vehicles produced and 486,532 delivered in Q3 2026. Arithmetic: deliveries exceeded production by 22,141. This difference alone is not a complete inventory reconciliation or proof of its causes. [S1]
- c2 — Model 3/Y accounted for 478,237 deliveries; the grouped Other Models figure was 8,295. The release does not split that group by vehicle model. [S1]
- c3 — Tesla’s Q3 2025 release reported 497,099 deliveries. Comparing the two releases gives 10,567 fewer deliveries, approximately 2.13% year-on-year decline. This is a delivery comparison, not revenue, profit or market-share growth. [S1,S2]
- c4 — Tesla reported 13.7 GWh of storage deployments, against 12.5 GWh a year earlier (arithmetic: +9.6%). GWh is deployed energy capacity, not revenue or electricity generated. [S1,S2]
- c5 — Tesla schedules Q3 financial results after U.S. market close October 21, 2026. The release says net income and cash flow come with earnings and cautions that delivery/deployment numbers alone do not establish quarterly financial performance. [S1]

Boundaries: No verified analyst consensus, stock-price response, incentive/price-discount effect, demand cause, country breakdown, completed Q3 profit result, Musk quotation or private motive.
