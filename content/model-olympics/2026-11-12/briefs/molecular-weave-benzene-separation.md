# A molecular weave and its next test

Source review, November 12, 2026. UT Austin's November 11 report explains research published in Nature on November 4. Researchers including collaborators at Zhejiang University and UT Austin describe a molecularly woven material for selective separation. The public abstract establishes the reported triaxial polymer architecture; the university supplies the accessible account of a benzene/cyclohexane test.

UT reports that the material took up 97.9% of the benzene from a 50/50 mixture. This result measures a captured component under that test. It establishes neither the purity of the recovered cyclohexane nor a percentage reduction in energy consumption. Four interwoven networks and three weaving directions describe different aspects of the structure.

The material self-assembles and can release benzene through heating for reuse, according to the university. Sessler's account of larger-scale work remains a participating researcher's report. UT says economic analysis still awaits completion.
