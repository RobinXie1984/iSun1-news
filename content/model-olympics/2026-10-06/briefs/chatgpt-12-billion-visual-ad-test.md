# ChatGPT visual ads: a planned US test, not a global launch
Event date: 2026-10-05. Collection date: 2026-10-06 (Hong Kong).

Verified claims:
[A1] OpenAI announced a visual advertising format on October 5. It says ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion people weekly. Treat that reach as a company claim, not independently audited ad impressions.
[A2] Testing is planned for later October in the US with an initial advertiser group, during image generation. Do not describe it as already launched worldwide.
[A3] OpenAI says ads will be labeled, separate from the generated image, and will not influence answers. These are its stated commitments, not independent findings of compliance or evidence of hidden paid answers.
[A4] It is expanding advertising measurement partnerships and developing controlled brand-suitability evaluation pilots. These pilots do not constitute completed independent certification.

Source for A1-A4: OpenAI, October 5, 2026, https://openai.com/index/new-chatgpt-ads-format-and-measurement/

Editorial tension to explore, explicitly as analysis: an intimate creative tool also becomes an advertising venue. Who pays for free access, and what will users tolerate? No invented rollout dates, individual user reactions, personal decisions by Sam Altman, or private conversations. No claims that every weekly user will see these ads.
