# A flowering desert, observed at different moments

Report date: November 19, 2026. Observations: September–October 2026, following austral winter rain. Editorial edition: November 20, 2026.

Parts of Chile’s Atacama Desert flowered after an unusually wet winter. NASA describes dormant seeds responding to water and scientists studying the bloom with satellites, drones and cameras. The positive development is a documented seasonal display of life and a useful opportunity to understand its variation. A temporary bloom does not establish permanent greening or a measured increase in long-term ecological resilience.

Landsat 8 observed the illustrated areas on September 14 and Landsat 9 on October 24. The Cistanthe field photograph is October 24; the Nolana field photograph is September 24.
