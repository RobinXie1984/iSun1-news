# Frozen factual brief — personalized prices and worker compensation inquiry
Collected 2026-10-29. FTC announcement October28: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/10/ftc-launches-personalized-pricing-compensation-inquiry
Primary15-page special-report template: https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/PP6b-Special-Order-Shell.pdf
F1 FTC ordered nine firms under Section6(b) of the FTC Act to supply information about personal data in pricing and compensation: Delta, United, JetBlue, Lyft, Uber, DoorDash, Instacart, Target and Walmart. Retailers are included; do not describe all nine as airlines. Andrew N. Ferguson is the chairman quoted in this announcement.
F2 Recipients have45days from receipt/service, not necessarily October28, to respond. The vote was2–0. Section6(b) permits broad studies without a specific law-enforcement purpose. This is an information-gathering inquiry, not a lawsuit, conviction, ban, fine or proof any named company secretly discriminated.
F3 FTC asks whether loyalty information, browsing or other personal data affects prices, and whether driver phone usage or acceptance history affects compensation. These are questions and possible practices, not established findings for every recipient. Distinguish demand-based dynamic pricing from person-specific pricing; do not assume all price differences use individual data.
F4 Template asks for pricing/offer-assignment/compensation methods, inputs, override procedures, tests, disclosures and division of customer payment between worker compensation and company revenue. General requests cover January1,2023 through compliance, with some research-project requests since2021. These are requested records, not a released company dataset or an account of actual company code.
F5 An August proposed policy statement is background, not a new enacted rule. Agency criticism of its2024study is attributed agency judgment; omit partisan rhetoric. Results, company responses and consumer/worker effects remain UNKNOWN. Explore transparency, bargaining and efficiency as clearly marked analysis. No fabricated affected customer, discrimination example, quote or private emotion; do not advise on evading a company or law.
