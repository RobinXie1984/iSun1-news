# An inspectable proof and one boundary of quantum advantage

Public source review, November9,2026. Physical Superintelligence (PSI) announced an AI-assisted proof of the Aaronson–Ambainis conjecture on November6. Its public repository provides a paper, formal statements, Lean proof material and verification records. A November7 exposition by Guy Blanc, Jordan Docter, Carmen Strassle and Li-Yang Tan explains the argument. Scott Aaronson discussed it on November8, saying he believed the proof correct because of the formal certificate and expert examination, while stating that he had yet to understand the proof himself.

The mathematical question concerns quantum query complexity: how often an algorithm accesses an input treated as a black-box information source. The consequence is scoped to yes/no problems on most Boolean inputs in the random-oracle setting, with polynomially related classical and quantum query counts. Query counts and total running time are different measures. The result does not make every quantum task classically easy or negate Shor’s factoring algorithm or quantum simulation of physics and chemistry.

PSI attributes discovery to its research system using a non-public Anthropic model. Its account of the run is a company-reported process claim. The exact model version, division of contribution between model and harness, discovery cost and full process reproducibility remain separate questions. Published verification notes also distinguish a formal proposition and proof term from the human judgment that the proposition expresses the intended conjecture. Our publication inspected source material but did not recompile the proof or check every mathematical step. The exposition is a scholarly explanation rather than a claimed journal referee report.
