# Mark Zuckerberg hires MongoDB's CEO after 322 days in the job
Edition date: October 4, 2026. Event: September 28, 2026; this is a follow-through story, not a new October 4 announcement.

Verified fact spine:
C1. Meta's September 28 announcement, attributed to Mark Zuckerberg, names CJ Desai Chief Enterprise Platform Officer, reporting directly to Zuckerberg. Meta is launching an enterprise AI platform; its stated initial products include Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API and Muse Code.
C2. MongoDB announced Desai's immediate departure that day and appointed former CEO Dev Ittycheria interim CEO, while seeking a permanent successor. It reaffirmed existing quarterly and annual guidance.
C3. MongoDB's November 3, 2025 announcement made Desai's CEO appointment effective November 10, 2025. Date subtraction to September 28, 2026 equals 322 elapsed days, under eleven months. This is our calculation, not a quoted tenure figure.
C4. Meta's announcement gives no hiring price, salary or guaranteed enterprise revenue. Do not invent a bidding war, motive, compensation or customer migration. Hiring a CEO does not mean buying his former company. Describe enterprise ambitions as plans, not achieved results.
