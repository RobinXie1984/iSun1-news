# What a resistance warning can tell us

WHO publication date: 25 November 2026. Surveillance reference year: 2025. CDC explanatory background has separate 2024–2026 dates.

WHO’s EGASP report follows gonococcal resistance in selected clinical settings across ten countries. The surveillance records 4,015 men, 4,123 episodes and 2,886 isolates receiving at least one antimicrobial susceptibility test. These counts refer to different stages of the observation chain. Drug-specific denominators vary.

Resistance to ceftriaxone and cefixime was detected in samples from Cambodia, India, Thailand and Viet Nam. These findings are laboratory signals, rather than counts of treatment failures or estimates of worldwide prevalence. Sentinel-clinic coverage, the populations sampled and testing methods shape the interpretation.

This independent feature uses a compact WHO factual account, CDC biological and laboratory background, and original measurement analysis. Infection detection, phenotypic susceptibility testing and treatment follow-up answer related but distinct questions. No personal treatment advice or unverified claim about current drug approvals is provided.
