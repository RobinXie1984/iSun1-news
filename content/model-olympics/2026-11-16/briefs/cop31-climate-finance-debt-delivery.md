# Climate finance: amount, terms and delivery

Speech date: November 13, 2026. Collected November 16, Hong Kong time.

At COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, UN Climate Change Executive Secretary Simon Stiell argued that affordable finance, simpler access and stronger national institutions could help countries break a debt-disaster cycle. His speech advocates coordinated delivery aligned with country priorities. It establishes his proposals and reasoning rather than a new award or completed reform.

The earlier COP29 goals distinguish USD300 billion annually by 2035, with developed countries taking the lead, from broader efforts by all actors to scale developing-country climate finance from public and private sources to USD1.3 trillion annually by 2035. These figures are distinct in scope and are not added together. Neither is a statement of money already disbursed in 2026.

Stiell supports grants and highly concessional finance for vulnerable economies alongside roles for private finance, loans and guarantees. He calls for simpler applications, strengthened domestic systems, and coordination of finance, technology and capacity-building. These proposals, and his positive-loop argument, require implementation and subsequent evidence of effects. Educational financing examples in the articles are hypothetical analysis.
