# Anthropic's ten-cent input tier
Announced October7,2026; collected October12. Do not call this today's launch.
H1. Anthropic introduced ClaudeHaiku5.5 for high-volume, cost-sensitive and speed-sensitive tasks, and as a subagent with larger Claude models. These are vendor positioning claims, not independent task-success evidence.
H2. Its launch price table lists per million tokens, for prompts up to100,000tokens versus over100,000: input $0.10/$0.50; output $0.50/$2.50; cache reads $0.01/$0.05; cache writes $0.125/$0.625. A million input tokens is a billing unit, not a permitted low-price single million-token prompt or a complete task for ten cents. Output and cache categories are separate.
H3. Anthropic says average run cost is roughly75% below Haiku4.5 and claims its fastest small model. This is its workload-dependent aggregate claim, not a guaranteed75% saving for every user. Haiku5.5 introduces adjustable effort; cost/intelligence tradeoffs still depend on workload.
H4. The vendor's OSWorld2.1 offline-subset table reports72.4% for Haiku5.5 vs15.7% Haiku4.5 and83.9% Sonnet5.5. These are reported benchmark scores, not live website success rates, safety guarantees or proof of unattended reliability. No independent reproduction by us.
Truthful conflict: cheap raw input changes volume economics, but per-success cost also depends on output, effort, retries and checking. Do not claim this account has monthly credits, change our production models, or invent Dario's personal quote/motive.
Unknown: our real task savings, paid invoices, customer deployment success, universal best model. No invented launch room, dialogue or private emotion.
