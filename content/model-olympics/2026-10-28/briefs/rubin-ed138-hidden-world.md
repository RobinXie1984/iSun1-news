# Frozen factual brief — Rubin's dwarf planet candidate
Collected 2026-10-28. Primary announcement dated 2026-10-27: https://rubinobservatory.org/news/rubin-first-dwarf-planet-candidate
F1 Rubin reports dwarf planet candidate 2026 ED138, identified by Matthew Holman using search code developed with Kevin Napier. It is beyond Pluto, currently about 46 astronomical units from the Sun.
F2 Rubin recorded it 30 times on 20 nights, March 4–July 11, 2026. Archival DECam images extend to 2016, constraining over ten years of orbit. Discovery was announced October 27; neither the observations nor the object originated that day.
F3 Estimated diameter is 600–1500 km, inferred from brightness, distance and estimated reflectivity. Infrared follow-up is needed to narrow size and confirm its nature. It is not a confirmed sixth recognized dwarf planet or Planet Nine.
F4 A crowded Milky Way field hid the moving point among many stars. Repeated imaging plus algorithms linked detections; archival recovery strengthened the orbit. Do not imply a resolved surface photograph or direct diameter measurement.
