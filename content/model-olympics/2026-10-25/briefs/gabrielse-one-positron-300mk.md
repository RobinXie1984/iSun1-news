# Frozen factual brief — one positron, a much sharper question
Collection 2026-10-25; Northwestern announcement and reported journal publication 2026-10-21. Earlier preprint first submitted 2026-05-03, latest v3 dated 2026-08-21. Do not call this an experiment performed today or the first antimatter ever trapped.
Sources:
S1 https://news.northwestern.edu/stories/2026/10/scientists-trap-and-tame-a-single-particle-of-antimatter-for-months?fj=1
S2 https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08147v3
S3 https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/gbkc-6nlj (journal page did not load in this check; journal/publication date reported by S1, DOI linked by paper record)
F1 Gerald Gabrielse's Northwestern team, with first author Thomas Myers, reports a one-positron quantum cyclotron: a single positron with its lowest cyclotron and spin energy levels resolved. A positron is the electron's antimatter counterpart. The advance is this quantum control, not first-ever creation or trapping of antimatter. S1/S2.
F2 Electric and magnetic fields in a Penning trap suspend it without contact with walls, inside a cryogenic vacuum enclosure at 300 millikelvins (0.3 kelvin above absolute zero). A 5.6-tesla field is used. A single positron has remained trapped for months. This is not a claim it remained in one unchanging excited quantum state for months. S1/S2.
F3 The team observes microwave-driven quantum transitions. S2 reports initial loading takes 8–11 hours in this simple apparatus; long storage makes infrequent reloading sufficient. Ground state preparation, state detection and controlled transitions create an experimental platform, not an antimatter power supply or commercial product. S2.
F4 Prior electron magnetic-moment measurement reached approximately one part in 10 trillion. An equivalent precision positron measurement and head-to-head comparison remain a future goal. No new positron magnetic-moment precision result or measured electron–positron discrepancy is established by this demonstration. S1/S2.
F5 Such comparison could test CPT symmetry in the lepton sector. A discrepancy would be consequential but has not been found here. Do not claim the Standard Model has been disproved, the matter–antimatter imbalance solved, or a multiverse discovered. The study authors' ambitions are not results. S1/S2.
F6 Positive reason: demonstrated control of individual antimatter quantum states creates a useful new route for testing fundamental physics; the payoff is a better experiment. Independent replication, the planned precision result, broader theory implications and any practical application remain open. Analysis may explore the value of patient measurement without inventing scenes, quotes, people or commercial benefits.
