# Navi Pillay wins the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize
Event: October9,2026. Verified for October10 Hong Kong collection.
[P1] The Norwegian Nobel Committee announced Navanethem “Navi” Pillay as the 2026 Peace Prize laureate for work promoting peace and international law.
Source: https://www.nobelpeaceprize.org/press/press-releases/nobel-peace-prize-for-2026
[P2] The official laureate account highlights her contribution to an international legal order and judicial rigour, integrity and courage. Those are the committee's assessment, not independently measured results.
Source: https://www.nobelpeaceprize.org/laureates/2026
[P3] OHCHR's September1,2008 historical notice records that the South African jurist assumed office as UN High Commissioner that day, previously an International Criminal Court judge. This is historical background, not a new appointment.
Source: https://cambodia.ohchr.org/en/news/new-high-commissioner-begins-work-geneva?page=1
