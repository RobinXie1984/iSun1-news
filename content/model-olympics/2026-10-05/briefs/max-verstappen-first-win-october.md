# Max Verstappen ends his 2026 wait for a win at Sepang
Edition date: October 5, 2026. Race date: October 4. FIA report: October 4; F1 report: October 4, 10:38 UTC.

Verified fact spine:
C1. Four-time world champion Max Verstappen won his and Red Bull's first Grand Prix of 2026. The FIA calls it the first victory for Red Bull Ford Powertrains. This is a first win of this season, not Verstappen's first career win. [S1, S3]
C2. Rain delayed proceedings; an interrupted start procedure caused another delay. Verstappen started from pole on intermediate tyres but lost the lead to Kimi Antonelli and George Russell at the standing start. He subsequently passed Russell. [S1, S3]
C3. A Safety Car following Valtteri Bottas's spin gave intermediate-tyre runners an opportunity to switch to slicks. Red Bull selected Soft tyres while Mercedes chose Mediums. After the restart, Verstappen passed Antonelli around the outside at Turn 1 and established a lead. These are observed race decisions, not proof that one tyre choice alone caused the victory. [S1, S3]
C4. Alex Albon's late stoppage brought a Virtual Safety Car, followed by a full Safety Car. Further stops and a compressed field put the lead at risk again. Verstappen remained ahead at the finish; Russell retired after completing 49 laps. Do not invent the cause of Russell's failure. [S1-S3]
C5. The official classification: 55 laps; Verstappen first, Antonelli second at +2.307 seconds, Lewis Hamilton third at +4.919 seconds. [S2]
C6. The official event was the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia, at Sepang. F1 announced that arrangement on July 26, 2026. Do not relocate the race to Bahrain or present July's announcement as new. [S4]

Story tension: a champion's long wait ends, but even pole position did not make this victory straightforward. Future wins and championship outcomes remain unknown. Do not invent private emotions, quotes, a championship comeback or a guaranteed engineering advantage.
