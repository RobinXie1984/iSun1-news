The Bottas Spin and the Strategic Crossroads

The race settled into a tense rhythm, with drivers managing their intermediate tyres on a track that was slowly beginning to dry, yet remained highly unpredictable in its micro-climates.

Red Bull opted to equip Verstappen with the Soft compound tyre. Mercedes, looking at the same data but arriving at a different conclusion, selected the Medium compound for their drivers.

Both choices had merit, and neither choice alone guarantees a victory without the driver executing a flawless performance on track.

For the race leader, a late-race Safety Car is a nightmare scenario. The meticulously constructed gap evaporates in an instant, and the field bunches up behind the leader. Furthermore, managing tyre temperatures and pressures while weaving behind the Safety Car is a delicate, high-stakes balancing act. The Soft tyres, which had been the key to Verstappen's restart dominance, now had to be kept in their optimal performance window while crawling at slow speeds.
