# Three confirmed cases, a wider transmission signal

Report date:23November2026. Initial illness dates:October2026. Confirmed count status:13November; later investigations through20November.

WHO reports three confirmed paralytic cVDPV1 cases from an initial four-case acute flaccid paralysis cluster in Al-Mayadin, Syria. Three contacts tested positive without acute flaccid paralysis. Twelve later paralysis reports remain under investigation. Genetic links establish community transmission; the emergence differs from February’s environmental detection. WHO describes vaccination, surveillance and health-service response measures. These observations establish urgency while retaining different confirmation categories.

CDC background explains why infection, paralysis and an investigated syndrome differ. Oral vaccine uses weakened live virus; prolonged circulation in insufficiently immunized communities can allow genetic changes. Inactivated vaccine contains no live virus. Vaccination remains central to prevention; this article provides public-health explanation rather than individual medical instructions or an estimate of hidden Syrian infections.
