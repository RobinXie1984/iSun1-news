# Greenland’s hidden Lake B
ESA reported October15,2026; collected October16. Underlying observations October2018–December2024, not a lake newly formed yesterday.
L1. ESA reports researchers inferred Lake B under western Flade Isblink, Northeast Greenland, using ICESat-2, Sentinel-1, ArcticDEM and climate modelling. They observed ice-surface movement, not a direct photograph through the ice.
L2. Peak surface uplift was about10m; drainage lowered it up to34cm/day. A bedrock depression reaches about600m BELOW SEA LEVEL, not600m of ice thickness. The recurring filling/drainage pattern had an exception in2022.
L3. Estimated storage up to0.37±0.11billion cubic metres, equivalent to63±23%of runoff in ITS CATCHMENT, not all Greenland. Discharge was delayed2–3months; storage is temporary, not permanent prevention of sea-level rise.
L4. Downstream Romer Sø volume changes suggest a connection, not a proved exclusive cause. Useful advance: seeing a hidden water pathway may improve freshwater predictions; future ecosystem impacts remain uncertain. Lead citation M.A.Kruse et al.; ESA article links Scientific Reports DOI10.1038/s41598-026-60541-7. Full paper inaccessible in this run; use ESA’s account, no invented fieldwork/scientist quotations.
