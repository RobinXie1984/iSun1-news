# The resources carried by a meal

Historical event: September 29, 2026. New archive feature first published November 27, 2026.

FAO and UNEP's seventh International Day of Awareness of Food Loss and Waste examined the transformative potential of reductions, including the relationship with forests and sustainable agrifood systems. The Rome event sat alongside COFO28 and World Forest Week. Institutional pages establish the theme and timing; the event recording stays linked at FAO.

UNEP/WRAP's March 2024 account estimates 1.05 billion tonnes of retail, food-service and household food waste in 2022, including inedible parts. Household waste represented 60% of that total. Those figures describe a dated downstream baseline. SDG12.3 separately aims to halve per-capita retail/consumer waste and reduce supply-chain losses by 2030.

EPA's environmental Wasted Food Scale gives priority to prevention and food's human-nourishment purpose. Its ranking covers environmental pathways; economic and social factors have separate assessments. Food's production and preparation use resources before disposal. The forest connection depends on actual production, purchasing and land-use responses. Original hypothetical examples explain these mechanisms, separate from measured outcomes of the observance.
