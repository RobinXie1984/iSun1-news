# Lando Norris's new team enters a larger junior grid
FIA announcement October 14, 2026; collected October 18. Future 2027 entry, not an F1 team entry.
F1. FIA and ACI selected all ten existing FIA Formula Regional European Championship teams plus Invicta Racing and LN4 Fusion for 2027: twelve teams, three cars each, 36 cars, six more than 2026.
F2. FIA identifies LN4 Fusion as recently established and co-founded by reigning F1 world champion Lando Norris, based in Italy's Veneto with a UK base. Other category expansion is a plan, not completed entry.
F3. This championship sits between FIA-certified Formula 4 and FIA Formula 3. It is not Formula 1, Formula 2 or a promise of promotion. All use Gen2 Tatuus T 326 cars and Pirelli tyres.
F4. The ten-event season is scheduled to begin April 8–11, 2027 in Portimao, Portugal. A Hungarian F1 support round remains subject to contract.
F5. More grid places are verified planned capacity, not proof of lower fees, funded scholarships, a confirmed driver lineup or wider socioeconomic access. Budgets, selection terms, Norris's investment amount and 2027 results are not disclosed here. FIA's development claims are the organiser's view.
