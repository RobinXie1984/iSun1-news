# Lisa Su, Fei-Fei Li and an $8.2 billion proposed deal
Original event/report date: 2026-09-28. Fact check: 2026-09-30 Hong Kong time.

[c1 / s1] AMD announced a definitive agreement to acquire World Labs, led by co-founder and CEO Fei-Fei Li. Lisa Su is AMD chair and CEO. The announced all-stock transaction is worth approximately $8.2 billion; closing is expected by the end of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and customary conditions.
[c2 / s1] After closing, Li would become AMD executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting to Su. World Labs works on spatial intelligence, including interactive 3D environments and robotic learning/simulation. AMD says model expertise will help shape its hardware, software and systems.
[c3 / s2] AMD's September 28 filing says the merger agreement was signed September 26. The eventual share count was not yet known: it depends on a ten-trading-day share-price average near closing, with customary adjustments.

Boundary: this is an announced, conditional acquisition, not a completed deal. $8.2 billion values the transaction, not Li's personal compensation. No verified rival bid, financial return, customer gain or specific motive is established. Worth the price is an editorial question, not a demonstrated investment conclusion.
