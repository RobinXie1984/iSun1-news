# The pressure concealed by a housing average

Publication date: 26 November 2026. Main observation year: 2025. Methodological background contains separately dated older figures, which are not relabelled as new data.

Eurostat’s Housing in Europe – 2026 edition reports an average housing-cost share of 19% of disposable income. For people below 60% of their national median income, the average share was 36%; for those at or above that threshold, it was 16%. These figures describe different income groups, rather than the same household’s change over time.

Housing-cost overburden measures the share of people living in households whose costs exceed 40% of disposable income, with housing-allowance adjustments. It differs from the average cost share and from purchase prices. Mortgage interest belongs in the cost measure; repayment of principal is a distinct cash commitment. Almost 10% of city residents and 5% of rural residents lived in overburdened households in 2025.
