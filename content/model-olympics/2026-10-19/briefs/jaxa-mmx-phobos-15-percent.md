# Japan's Mars-moon sample plan and NASA's 15% share
NASA report October15,2026, page updatedOctober16; collectedOctober19. Planned mission, not accomplished sample return.
M1. JAXA's MMX aims to study Phobos/Deimos and return Phobos material. NASA reports a targeted launch from Tanegashima at15:41EDT October19 (04:41JST October20;03:41HKT October20). At collection this is still a target, not verified launch success.
M2. Origin hypotheses include captured ancient asteroids or debris from an impact on Mars. Samples/measurements may discriminate; the origin is not settled here and no life discovery is claimed.
M3. NASA supplies MEGANE gamma-ray/neutron spectroscopy to determine elemental composition, a nitrogen-burst pneumatic surface sampler complementing JAXA's deeper coring sampler, and Deep Space Network support.
M4. NASA says it will obtain/curate about15% of the total returned sample at Johnson Space Center. That is a planned share, not15% of a moon, ownership of Mars or an achieved delivery. Total sample mass, mission cost and commercial payoff are not established here.
M5. NASA's timeline: Mars arrivalAugust2027; Phobos sampling no earlier thanDecember2028; Earth delivery2031. These are forward plans. NASA science chiefNickyFox describes the sample-curation partnership; no invented quotes, motives, launch outcome or returned findings.
