# Which effort helps people learn together?

Report date: November 17, 2026. Editorial edition: November 18, 2026.

MIT describes work connecting artificial intelligence, society and the humanities. Its earlier campus-report observations include less time in office hours and less work on problem sets with peers. The account gives no sample design, controlled causal estimate or quantified learning effect; it is not a new November 17 experiment proving that AI damages cognition.

MITHIC launched in 2024. Since then, the whole initiative supported 67 projects across 31 MIT units with US$5.5 million; roughly a third of projects concern AI, society and humanity. These figures do not describe an annual AI budget, 31 universities or 67 completed cognitive experiments.

Graham Jones and Arvind Satyanarayan helped launch Humane User Experience Design, distinguishing systems that serve humans from systems that merely appear humanlike. Concerns about dependence do not establish a measured addiction rate.
