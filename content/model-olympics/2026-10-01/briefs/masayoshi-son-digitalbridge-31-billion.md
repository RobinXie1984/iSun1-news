# Masayoshi Son's SoftBank closes a $3.1 billion DigitalBridge acquisition
Event date: September 30, 2026 (U.S. time). Verified October 1, 2026 Hong Kong time.

[c1 / s1] SoftBank Group announced that it completed the acquisition of all DigitalBridge outstanding common stock for approximately US$3.1 billion. DigitalBridge became a controlled subsidiary, remaining a separately managed platform led by Marc Ganzi.
[c2 / s2] The original December 29, 2025 agreement described approximately US$4.0 billion in enterprise value and US$16 cash per common share. These figures measure different things: do not call the $3.1 billion completion figure a price cut from $4 billion.
[c3 / s2] Masayoshi Son is SoftBank Group's chairman and CEO. SoftBank's stated rationale was to expand the infrastructure needed for AI: DigitalBridge invests in data centers, cell towers, fiber networks and edge infrastructure. The buyer's promised benefits are not independently established results.
[c4 / s3] DigitalBridge announced on September 22 that it had received the required regulatory approvals, including FERC and CFIUS. That was the approvals milestone; September 30 is the reported completion milestone.

Boundary: SoftBank bought the asset manager's common equity. This is not personal spending by Son, ownership of every asset its funds manage, or proof of AI returns. Do not invent capacity, energy savings, layoffs, personal emotions or an investment recommendation. The earlier $4 billion enterprise value and latest $3.1 billion common-stock figure are not interchangeable.
