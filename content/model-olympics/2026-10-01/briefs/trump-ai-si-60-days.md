# Trump orders the executive branch to rename AI; a definition proposal is due in 60 days
Event date: September 29, 2026. Verified October 1, 2026 Hong Kong time.

[c1 / s1, section 2] President Donald Trump signed an order directing U.S. executive departments and agencies, to the extent allowed by law, to replace Artificial Intelligence/AI with Super Intelligence/SI in official communications, websites, reports and other non-statutory documents.
[c2 / s1, section 3(a)] For this order, SI presently covers the same technologies as the existing statutory AI definition in 15 USC 9401(3), unless law provides otherwise. The order is evidence of a naming policy, not scientific proof that machines have achieved superintelligence.
[c3 / s1, section 3(b)] The president's science and technology assistant must submit proposed legislative language for a federal SI definition within 60 days. A proposal is not an enacted law, and this is not a 60-day deadline for machines to become smarter.
[c4 / s1, section 2(b)] The order does not require alteration of earlier regulations, presidential actions, contracts, grants or historical documents. It is an executive-branch instruction, not a ban on everyone using AI.
[c5 / s2] The accompanying White House fact sheet frames the change as recognizing technological progress. This is the administration's stated rationale, not an independently tested capability finding.
