# A budget proposal with several baselines

On November 21, 2026, European Council President António Costa presented a proposed negotiating compromise for the EU’s 2028–34 long-term budget. Its current-price commitment total is about €1.781 trillion, roughly €204 billion below the Commission’s original proposal. The draft’s constant-2025-price commitment ceiling is about €1.583 trillion; these price bases stay separate.

Horizon Europe would receive €140 billion at 2025 prices, compared with about €89.3 billion in the current framework and €154.9 billion in the Commission proposal. The same proposed allocation can therefore rise against one baseline and fall against another. Costa’s average national-contribution share moves from 0.80% to 0.82% of GNI, a difference of 0.02 percentage points.

Five proposed revenue streams and changes to existing resources support an estimated annual funding contribution. The detailed draft specifies non-collected electronic waste. Revenue estimates, commitment ceilings and paid cash remain different quantities. November 26–27 discussions, unanimous Council adoption after Parliament’s consent, and national own-resource procedures remain future stages.
