# Two telescope beams, one glass chip

NSF’s November 19, 2026 update highlights a successful real-sky engineering demonstration of CHARIOT, a photonic testbench at Georgia State University’s CHARA Array on Mount Wilson. The chip combines light from two telescopes; the array contains six. It replaces part of the beam-combining optics while the collecting telescopes, beam transport and other infrastructure remain.

The original university report dates from October 27, and the authors’ preprint from September 28. Observations occurred in May and October 2024 and July 2025. Bright-star measurements reached sub-percent relative precision in fringe squared visibility; fainter targets and between-measurement stability have different limits. Fibre-connected chips can be exchanged within hours with optical adjustments. Planet-science uses and improved faint-star precision remain goals.
