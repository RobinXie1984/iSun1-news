# SPLICE: a tested Earth demonstration and a future landing problem

Public source review, November6,2026. NASA’s November4 report states that an Alta-X drone carrying SPLICE completed simulated lunar descent/landing maneuvers on August27 at Rogers Dry Lake near Armstrong Flight Research Center, California. The payload mapped terrain, assessed landing areas and diverted when the target was judged unsafe. NASA Johnson developed the payload and Armstrong specialists supported integration and flight testing.

The achievement is an Earth drone demonstration. Exact landing error, success fraction, flight duration, numeric readiness level and lunar mission certification were not supplied. Software-catalog release remained an attributed plan in the announcement, not verified current availability.
